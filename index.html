Mike “Triggz” Ramos & Anthony “Ant” Ramos are brothers who were born and raised in San Francisco. Growing up, their passion for art was evident; ranging from drawing, script, airbrushing, graffiti and painting. Mike was even fortunate enough to take part in painting murals in a couple SF neighborhoods.

Growing up, they spent a lot of time around the family tattoo business. Their uncle co-owned a tattoo shop that was located in the Mission District for over two decades. Naturally, their love for the art of tattooing began to blossom. When they became of age, Anthony & Mike were both apprenticed under their Uncle and Dad, and worked at the family tattoo business for many years.

With each tattoo they incorporate what the client is looking for, while adding their personal twist, and creating a custom masterpiece. They take pride in each piece they do. With their limitless talent, love for art and their drive to succeed, it was only a matter of time until they opened up a tattoo shop of their own. This is Mike and Ant’s dream, hence the name, Street Dreams Tattoo Studio. We are also excited to announce an addition to our Street Dreams family, Jose Lecue & Ramon Reyes.

  1. Remove the dressing after a minimum of one hour. You do not need to re-bandage.
  2. Wash the tattoo gently with anti-bacterial soap and warm water.
  3. Pat / dab to dry the tattoo gently.
  4. Apply a small amount of unscented lotion over the entire tattoo, 2–3X a day, until fully healed (4–6 weeks).
  5. You will most likely develop a protective scab. Do not pick or scratch at the scab, even if it itches. The entire thing will fall off on its own. This is imperative to protect the color and design.
  6. You may take a short shower with your new tattoo, but don’t soak the tattoo for too long in any kind of water. This means no baths, hot tubs or swimming.
  7. Avoid exposing your new tattoo to sunlight as much as possible, but especially during the healing process.

Touch-ups: pricing depends on how the tattoo was cared for and is determined by your artist once the piece is fully healed (about 4–6 weeks). Come into the studio and speak with your artist to schedule one.

01What if I need a touch-up?

The aftercare of your tattoo will determine whether or not you will need a touch-up. If needed, prices on touch-ups are based on the care of your tattoo and will be determined by the artist once the tattoo is completely healed (about 4–6 weeks). To schedule a touch-up, you must come in the studio and speak with your artist. Please see our aftercare instructions.

02I am under 18 and have permission from my parent/guardian to get a tattoo. Is that okay?

Absolutely not.

04How do I make an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcomed every day, although it is best to call the studio & set up a date where you can come in for a consultation with your artist. At that time, a deposit will be needed to book your appointment. This deposit will go towards the price of your tattoo. Deposits are non-refundable. If you need to reschedule your appointment, please call us within 72 hours of your appointment to keep your deposit valid. This is essential to keeping our time management effective for both the artist and the clients.

05Is there parking?

Our studio is located on a semi-busy street with Muni street cars passing thru. There is plenty of street parking on San Jose Ave. that is non-metered.
